# Cleaning Quality Tracker

A standalone WordPress plugin for commercial cleaning companies to document inspections, assign corrective tasks, and generate client-facing quality reports.

## 🎯 The Business Problem

Commercial cleaning companies manage recurring office contracts. When a client complains about a missed task, the cleaning company has no consistent record of inspections, evidence, or corrective work. This leads to disputes, lost contracts, and lack of accountability.

## 💡 The Solution

*   **Reusable Checklists:** Admins create inspection templates per site.
*   **Supervisor Portal:** Supervisors log in and fill out inspections, recording scores and attaching photo evidence.
*   **Corrective Tasks:** Failed checks automatically create rework tasks assigned to staff.
*   **Client Reports:** Generate a PDF quality report for the client.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

*   **WordPress:** Custom Post Types, Roles, and Capabilities
*   **PHP:** OOP Architecture
*   **JavaScript:** AJAX for dynamic form submission
